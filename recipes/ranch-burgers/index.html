Ranch Burgers & Fries

A simple burger-and-fries dinner seasoned with Crooked Gate No. 01 Ranch from the patties to the potatoes.

Make Dinner Meet No. 01 Ranch
What's for Dinner?

One Blend. The Whole Meal.

No. 01 Ranch seasons both the burgers and the potatoes, so you can build the whole dinner around one bag from the pantry.

The Game Plan

Get the fries going first. Season and cook the burgers while the potatoes crisp up. Build the burgers, pull the fries, and dinner is done.

Grab This

Ingredients

Ground beef
Potatoes, cut into fries
Crooked Gate No. 01 Ranch
Cooking oil
Favorite cheese
Burger buns or crisp lettuce
Favorite burger toppings

Step 01

Get the Fries Going.

Cut the potatoes into fries. Toss them with a little cooking oil and Crooked Gate No. 01 Ranch until evenly coated.

Air fry or bake until browned and crisp, turning or tossing them as needed.

Step 02

Season the Burgers.

Form the ground beef into patties and season both sides with No. 01 Ranch.

Cook the patties in a hot skillet or on the grill until they reach your preferred doneness.

Step 03

Finish the Burgers.

Add cheese near the end of cooking if you're using it, then let it melt over the patties.

Build each burger on a toasted bun or keep it lighter with crisp lettuce.

Step 04

Make It Yours.

Add avocado, pickles, peppers, onions, cheese, or whatever your family likes on a burger.

Pile the Ranch fries alongside it and get dinner on the table.
