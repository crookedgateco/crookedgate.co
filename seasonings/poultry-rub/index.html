Family Recipe · No. 02

Poultry Rub

A savory blend built for chicken, turkey, potatoes, roasted vegetables and the kind of weeknight dinners that don't need to be complicated to taste good.

Choose Your Bag
What's In It

Simple Ingredients. Big Flavor.

Garlic, onion, salt, parsley, smoke flavor, mustard powder, pepper, paprika, jalapeño and citric acid.

Good On

Chicken. Turkey. Potatoes. Green beans. Roasted vegetables. Rice. And whatever else needs a savory kick before dinner hits the table.

Season the Main

Start With the Chicken.

Coat the chicken with No. 02 before it hits the heat. Garlic, herbs, pepper and a little smoke build into the meat while it cooks.

Then Hit the Sides

Don't Stop at the Chicken.

No. 02 works just as well on the rest of the plate. Toss it with green beans, potatoes or whatever vegetables are going alongside dinner.

From Our Table

Poultry Rub Chicken Dinner

Seasoned chicken, green beans and pasta. One blend working across the plate for an easy dinner that tastes like somebody actually had a plan.

Make It Yours

One Blend. Plenty of Dinners.

Use No. 02 on chicken breasts, thighs, wings or a whole bird. Bring it over to potatoes and vegetables and you've got one seasoning working across the entire meal.
